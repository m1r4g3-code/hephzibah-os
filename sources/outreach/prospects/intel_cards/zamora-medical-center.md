THE SCRIPT
──────────────────────────────────────────

OPEN WITH THIS:

  "Dr. Zamora — your 3.9-star rating isn't a doctor problem.
   Every single negative review you have is describing an admin
   experience — phone calls not answered, hard to reach, long waits.
   That's a process bottleneck, and I build the systems that fix it.
   Give me 90 seconds."

──────────────────────────────────────────

ONCE THEY'RE TALKING — ask these one at a time, wait for the answer:

  "How are patients booking right now — mostly by phone?"

  "What's the most time-consuming thing your front desk deals
   with every single day?"

  "When they're tied up on those calls — who's covering the
   phones and walk-ins at the same time?"

  "When a patient no-shows with no notice — does that slot get
   filled, or does it just go empty?"

  "If reminders were automated and patients could book online
   at any hour — what would that actually change for you?"

──────────────────────────────────────────

AFTER THEY'VE ANSWERED — say this:

  "What you're describing is exactly what I build — an automation
   layer that pulls all of that manual work out of your staff's hands."

──────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT YOU DO (say this once, then stop):

  "I build custom intake and reminder systems — patients book online,
   reminder texts go out automatically, intake routes into your workflow
   without anyone touching it. Practices cut no-shows 20-30% and reclaim
   2 hours of front desk time per day. I build it myself, not a template."

──────────────────────────────────────────

CLOSE WITH THIS:

  "I do a free 20-minute audit — I look at your intake and reminder
   flow and show you exactly what can be automated and what the dollar
   impact is for your volume. Are you free Thursday afternoon, or does
   Friday morning work better?"

──────────────────────────────────────────


WHEN THEY PUSH BACK

   What they don't usually touch is the reminder and intake workflow

   Quick question: what's your no-show rate on a typical week?"

──────────────────────────────────────────

IF "I'd have to talk to my office manager":

  ⚠ DO NOT say "okay no problem" — that's the end of the deal.

  "That's exactly the right move — the office manager is honestly
   the one who'd feel the biggest difference day to day. Would it
   make more sense to get on a quick call with both of you together,
   or brief them first and call me back with a time that works?"

──────────────────────────────────────────

IF "We're not looking for anything right now":

  "I hear that — I'd just ask: those 3.9 stars aren't going up on
   their own. Every month without a change, that rating stays flat
   or gets worse. The cost of waiting isn't zero. Is 20 minutes to
   see the actual number worth it before you decide?"
