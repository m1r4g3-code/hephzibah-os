# Competitor Tracking

Who else is selling to the same buyer, what they do well, and where the opening is.

**The rule:** track competitors to find the gap, never to copy the price. Copying prices is how a market becomes a commodity market, and Hephzibah loses a commodity market on day one — a Computer Village stall has lower overheads, more stock, and no brand to protect.

---

## Competitor Archetypes

Four kinds of seller exist in this market. Each is beaten differently.

### 1. The Computer Village Stall
Physical shop, walk-in trade, no online presence beyond a WhatsApp number.
- **Strength:** the buyer can hold the device. Instant availability. Deep stock.
- **Weakness:** no records, no warranty that survives the buyer leaving, no way to verify anything they say, no reachable follow-up.
- **The gap:** documented condition and a seller who still answers the phone in three months.

### 2. The Instagram Gadget Page
20k–200k followers, high-volume posting, heavy emoji, "DM for price."
- **Strength:** reach and social proof. Fast, familiar buying flow.
- **Weakness:** "DM for price" is friction that filters out serious buyers. Volume model means minimal inspection. Reputation is one bad thread away from gone.
- **The gap:** price in the listing, specifics in the caption, and a feed that looks like a specialist instead of a market stall.

### 3. The Verified Retailer
Slot, Pointek, 3CHub — physical stores, real warranties, formal.
- **Strength:** genuine trust. Real warranty. Card payment. Actual returns.
- **Weakness:** 15–30% more expensive. Little to no used-device business. Impersonal.
- **The gap:** most of their trust at a meaningfully lower price, plus the used market they will not touch.

### 4. The Individual Reseller
One person on X or WhatsApp selling a handful of units.
- **Strength:** cheapest. Personal. Often genuinely honest.
- **Weakness:** no consistency, no stock depth, disappears when they get a job.
- **The gap:** be the one that is still there next quarter with stock.

**Where Hephzibah actually sits:** the trust of archetype 3 at closer to the price of archetype 1, with the reach of archetype 2. That is the whole position. It is only defensible while the inspection discipline holds.

## Competitive Review — Monthly

First of the month, 20 minutes:

1. Pull current prices from each tracked competitor on the top 3 anchor SKUs.
2. Update the price position line with the date.
3. Note any change in categories, cadence, or positioning.
4. Ask the only question that matters: **has anyone started closing the trust gap?**
   If a competitor begins publishing battery health, named flaws, and inspection photos, the Hephzibah moat is under attack and it needs a `/war-room`. Nothing else a competitor does is an emergency.
5. Append anything material to `market/intelligence.md` with a **So what** line.
