# Insights — Weekly Synthesis

One entry per week. Written by the OS during `/daily-brief` on the last session of the week, or on demand.

This is not a summary of what happened — `_SESSION.md` already holds that. This is **what the week means**: what the numbers changed, what a decision revealed, what should be done differently. An insights file that reads like a diary has failed.

---

## Entry Format

```markdown
### Week of YYYY-MM-DD

**Numbers:** [units] units · ₦[revenue] · [margin]% avg · [days] days to sell · [returns] returns

**What worked:** [specific. a product, a channel, a listing angle, a price move — with the evidence]

**What did not:** [specific. name it plainly.]

**What we learned:** [the transferable thing. the part that changes a future decision.]

**What changes next week:** [one or two concrete actions. not aspirations.]

**Pattern watch:** [anything that has now happened twice. third time promotes it to market/patterns/.]
```

---

## Synthesis Questions

Run these against the week. Skip the ones with no evidence — an invented answer is worse than a blank.

**Money**
- Which SKU produced the most gross profit? Was it the one expected?
- Which SKU consumed the most capital for the least return?
- Did any margin land below the 35% floor? Which, and why?

**Velocity**
- What sold fastest? What is the shared trait with the last fast seller?
- What has been sitting longest? At what point does it become a decision?

**Demand**
- What did buyers ask for that was not in stock? *(This is the single most valuable signal the business generates and it is the easiest one to lose — it arrives in DMs and disappears.)*
- What question got asked three or more times? That is next week's content.

**Trust**
- Any returns or complaints? What did the condition report say versus what came back?
- Any referral sales? Who sent them, and were they thanked?

**Discipline**
- Was any gate broken this week? Which, and what was the justification at the time?
- Did anything in `sourcing` sit past 14 days?
- Did any approved listing sit unpublished past 7 days?

That last block matters most. Gates get broken gradually and with good reasons, and the only defence is writing down each breach while the reason still sounds convincing.
