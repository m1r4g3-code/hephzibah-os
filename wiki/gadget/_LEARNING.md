# Learning Ledger

**The OS is in its learning phase and should behave like it.** Emmanuel has run this business; the OS has not. Almost every number in this domain was reasoned from market structure rather than from what actually happens at Ikeja, and two of those guesses have already been caught and corrected by him.

This file is the open list of what is still unknown, ranked by how badly the gap distorts an answer. It is not a homework assignment — it gets filled in whenever a fragment arrives, in whatever order suits him.

**How knowledge arrives:** a text or voice note to the Telegram bot, any time. `intake.py` drops it in `sources/lessons/YYYY-MM-DD.md` raw and unparsed. Claude promotes what matters into the nodes below and records where it went.

**Standing rule while this file has open BLOCKING items:** when the OS does not know something, it says so. It does not produce a confident number with nothing behind it — a stated gap is cheap and a fabricated figure gets acted on.

## BLOCKING — the OS gives wrong answers without these

### 1. Real price ranges
The single biggest gap. Without it every demand and margin score is guesswork wearing a number.

For the models he actually moves — iPhone 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 15, Samsung S-series, whatever else:
- What does it **cost** from Matte, and from Yemi's group?
- What does it **actually sell for** to a buyer, after negotiation?
- How fast does that move — weekly, monthly, only on FX swings?

→ `market/price-ranges.md` *(not created yet — waiting on real numbers rather than a scaffold of blanks)*

### 2. Why one vendor is cheaper than another
Emmanuel offered to teach this and it is the highest-value thing in the ledger. It is the difference between a bargain and a trap, and it is exactly the judgement the OS cannot reason its way to.

- What makes a phone cheap — grade, battery, no box, replaced screen, vendor's own cost, urgency to sell?
- When is a low price a **warning** rather than an opportunity?
- What does Matte's own stock price like versus his boss's?

→ `market/vendor-dynamics.md`

### 3. The buyers who actually have money
Now the central question. Customers come but cannot pay. So:
- Who are the ones who **do** buy? Age, work, what they use the phone for.
- Where did those specific buyers come from?
- What do the low-budget ones ask for, and at what price would they actually close?

→ `market/buyer-segments.md`

## IMPORTANT — would materially sharpen the scoring

**Condition language as his market uses it.** "UK used" is unregulated. What does it mean when *Matte* says it? Grade A from a vendor versus grade A in a listing. Which faults are normal and which are dealbreakers locally.

**What kills a deal.** The specific moment buyers walk. Price? A flaw? Slow reply? Not trusting him yet?

**Negotiation reality.** How much do buyers actually ask off? What does he normally concede? Does the 5–8% built-in room match what happens?

**Deal flow.** Start to finish, what actually happens and where it goes wrong.

**Competition.** Who else sells to these buyers, at what price, and why do buyers pick them.
