Under financial pressure, decisions distort toward desperation. The ₦50k → ₦125k situation — the impulse toward betting rather than building is a symptom, not a character flaw. It's the brain under scarcity switching to short-term survival mode. Building even a small financial buffer changes the quality of every strategic decision made from that position.
