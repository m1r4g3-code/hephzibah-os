Medical practices lose significant revenue and staff hours to manual admin: phone tag for appointment scheduling, paper intake forms, no online booking system, unmanaged review responses, manual patient follow-up. Doctors don't have admin problems — they have systems problems wearing an admin costume. The 3.9-star rating is the visible scar. The missing booking form is the bleeding wound.
