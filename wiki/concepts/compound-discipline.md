Daily habits only compound when they're actually daily. Not mostly daily. Not when motivated. Not when conditions are right. Every day. The morning routine, the dead hangs, the reading, the 10 outreach messages — skipped one day, the streak resets. The compound interest of identity habits is either building or it isn't. There's no partial credit.
