Zero testimonials on Contra. Low review counts on prospect companies. Absence of external validation creates distrust from both sides of the transaction — as a seller and as a buyer. The gap is the same: someone did work, someone received value, no one documented it publicly. Closing this gap is the single highest-leverage move on Contra and the most exploitable pain signal in company research.
