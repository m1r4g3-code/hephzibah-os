Sequential lookahead under perfect information — map the move, their most likely countermove, your response, several layers deep, before acting. Best used when the situation is genuinely a sequence of moves both sides can see (a scope conversation, a pricing change, a public post) and the goal is picking the opening move that's still good three moves from now, not just good right now. Blind spot: assumes you can see the whole board. Real negotiations rarely offer that — pair with [[poker-under-uncertainty]] whenever the other side's actual position or intent is unclear, not just their possible responses.
