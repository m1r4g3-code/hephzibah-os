# LinkedIn Caption Framework

Active standard for every caption Emmanuel posts. Not a template — a set of constraints.

---

## The Hook (the only thing that matters first)

LinkedIn shows ~3 lines before "see more." Both lines must do work.

**Line 1:** starts with a specific number or action word. Never "I." Creates immediate curiosity or tension.
**Line 2:** short. Creates irony, counterintuitive pull, or deepens the tension. Forces the read.

Together they are the pattern interrupt. If they don't stop the scroll, nothing else matters.

Pass test: "Would someone stop mid-scroll for this?" If no — rewrite. Do not proceed.

### Hook patterns that work

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Specific number + unexpected result | "Found 11 hours of automatable work in a 20-minute audit last week." |
| Short action + irony reveal | "The client thought her team was just slow. / They weren't." |
| Counterintuitive claim + tension | "3 clients. Same problem. None of them knew it was a problem." |
| Direct observation + specific detail | "Posted at 8AM. 847 impressions. One DM. Here's what I changed." |

### Hook patterns that fail

- Starts with "I" or "We"
- Generic observation ("Your team might be missing something")
- Any opener that fits any post about any topic

---

## AI Slop — Kill List

Any of these present = rewrite before output:

**LinkedIn clichés:**
Full stop. / Genuinely curious / Let's be honest / Here's what nobody tells you / The dirty little secret / In today's world / Game changer / Not many people talk about this / This is why I do what I do

**Corporate register:**
systems gap / productivity problem / operational efficiency / streamline / leverage / synergy / robust / cutting-edge / seamless

**Structural tells:**
- More than 1 parallel triplet (three items, same grammatical structure in a row)
- A sentence that would look good as a carousel graphic or tweet (quotable aphorism = AI wrote it)
- Neat 3-part structure that resolves too cleanly (intro, body, summary)
- Flat tone with no register drift

---

## Body Rules

- **Register drift:** tone must shift at least twice. Fragments. Frustrated aside. Short sentence after a long one. Sudden opinion. No flat corporate tone start to finish.
- **One specific operational detail:** something that broke, took longer, or surprised. Proof it's real experience.
- **No parallel triplets.** Max 2 similar items in a row.
- **No neat summary.** End on a question or abrupt observation. Never wrap up.

---

## CTA and Ending

- **Carousel posts:** "Swipe through." One line. Then question.
- **Engagement question:** blunt and real. "What's the most repetitive thing your team does?" Not "I'd love to hear your thoughts."
- **No hashtags in caption body.** First comment only, within 60 seconds of posting.

---

## First Comment (post within 60 seconds)

Hashtags + portfolio/Contra link. No selling text. Just tags and link.

---

## Applying Deep Research

Relevant frameworks that apply to LinkedIn captions:

- **Challenger Sale (CLAUDE.md #54):** Teach something new. Reframe before pitching. The hook should teach, not sell.
- **SPIN Implication questions (#60):** The body should make the reader feel the consequence of inaction, not just describe the problem.
- **Dunford positioning (#69):** Know what the reader's alternative is (doing nothing, ignoring it). The caption competes against that inertia.
