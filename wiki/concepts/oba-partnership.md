## Division of Labour — Crisis Operations

When a platform goes down or a client situation escalates:

1. **Emmanuel calls the shots on technical and strategy** — what to do, in what order, what to say
2. **Oba executes client-facing communication** — he knows the clients personally, his voice is warmer
3. **No parallel outreach** — one person per client. Decide who, then go. Never both at same time.
4. **Sync point every 30 minutes** — "I handled X, you handle Y, meet back at 12:30"

The July 2026 crisis showed what happens without this: both people messaging in parallel, confusion about which assets are where, duplicate effort, missed clients, midnight stress.

## Communication Protocol Under Pressure

When Oba is overwhelmed:
- Read the signals: "my head is hot" = he's at capacity. Stop adding tasks.
- Give him one clear instruction, not three.
- "Oba, just send [specific message] to [specific person]. I'll handle the rest."
- Let him step back when he needs to. He said it: "trust you please go ahead." That's the handoff.

When Emmanuel is overwhelmed:
- Write it out. Voice notes are fine with Oba.
- One task at a time in WhatsApp. Not a wall of text.
- If the decision is complex, say "let me think on this and come back to you."

General rule: **crisis + multiple tasks in one WhatsApp message = nothing gets done correctly.** One message, one task, one person.

## How to Get Client Contacts from Oba

Never wait until crisis. Ask at first mention of a new client.

Template:
> "Oba, [client name] — do you have their direct email? I want to file it in case we ever need it."

Oba almost always has it. He just doesn't think to share it unless asked. Ask every time.

## Financial Clarity

The 50/50 split applies to Oba-referred clients only.
On Emmanuel's own Upwork account: 100% Emmanuel's. Oba is not part of that.
On direct clients Emmanuel sources: 100% Emmanuel's unless Oba is involved in delivery.

This should be explicit and agreed upfront on any new client. Ambiguity about splits creates tension. Say the number before the project starts.
