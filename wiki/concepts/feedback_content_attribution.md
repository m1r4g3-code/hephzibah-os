Use "we" in LinkedIn/content copy when describing client work that involved [[oba]] (Adelaja Obanijesu) — he brings and manages client relationships (SERAMAN/Giovanni, 50/50 split), Emmanuel builds the pipeline. Describing that work as solo "I built" erases a real partner.

**Why:** Direct correction from Emmanuel while reviewing draft LinkedIn copy — he collaborated with Obanijesu on the work being described and wants that reflected.

**How to apply:** In project-narrative copy (the "what we built/what broke" story), use "we." In personal positioning/bio lines (headline, first-comment tagline like "I build automation pipelines for product brands") keep "I" — that's Emmanuel's standing brand statement, not a claim about one specific build. Check whether Oba was actually involved in the specific project before defaulting to "we" — don't apply blindly to fully solo work.
