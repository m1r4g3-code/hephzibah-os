A cold opener that mentions a specific number, year, or observation sounds like insider knowledge. One that doesn't sounds like a template. "Your 3.9 stars" lands differently than "your reviews." "Your copyright year is 2013" lands differently than "your website is outdated." Specificity is the mechanism that disarms the scam-sensor and creates the feeling: *this person actually looked.*
