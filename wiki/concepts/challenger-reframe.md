The move that turns a cold call into a consultation. Name something true about the prospect's business that they feel but haven't said out loud — not an accusation, a diagnosis. "Your 3.9 stars aren't a doctor problem. They're an admin problem." When this lands, the prospect stops evaluating you and starts thinking about themselves. That's the moment the call shifts.
