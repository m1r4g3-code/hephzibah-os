Before committing to a plan, attack it yourself — what would guarantee this fails, what am I not seeing, who does this move actually benefit. Charlie Munger's "invert, always invert," and the military/corporate practice of red-teaming your own strategy before the other side gets the chance to. This is the check that catches a reflexively helpful move before it ships: offering a client's partner a polished self-service walkthrough felt generous in the moment — inverting it ("what does this cost me later") would have shown it quietly hands away the one thing protecting the maintenance leverage. Run this pass on any recommendation involving real money, scope, or a relationship already in a delicate state, before it goes out, not after someone else catches it.
