Know what you do if the deal doesn't happen, before you walk into the conversation about the deal. A negotiator who hasn't worked out their own fallback accepts worse terms than they needed to, because every ask from the other side feels like it has to be absorbed rather than weighed against a real alternative. Before raising scope or pricing with a client — what's the actual fallback if they say no or go quiet? If that's not answered yet, that's the homework to do before the conversation, not during it.
