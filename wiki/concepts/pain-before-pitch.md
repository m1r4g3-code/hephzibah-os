The prospect has to confirm the problem before you name the solution. Pitching before discovery creates resistance — they're defending against your offer instead of examining their own problem. The sequence is always: situation → problem → implication → need-payoff → then and only then: pivot. Skipping this is the single most common reason calls die in the first 60 seconds.
