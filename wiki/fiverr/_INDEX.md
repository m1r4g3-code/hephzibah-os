## Platform Risk Rules

1. **Off-platform contact is mandatory** — capture email, LinkedIn, Instagram at first message. Never rely solely on Fiverr DM.
2. **Never do AI video of real public figures** — permanent ban trigger, no exceptions, no client pressure overrides this.
3. **Client threatening bad review ≠ reason to comply** — a bad review is survivable. Account suspension is not.
4. **Never cover tool costs during "test periods"** — bake all tool costs into the retainer.
5. **50% deposit before any build starts** — Fiverr milestone system or do not proceed.
