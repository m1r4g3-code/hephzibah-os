## Primary Vision: AI Video Editing Webapp

**The problem:** Video editing is slow, skill-dependent, and template-driven. No existing tool actually understands story, emotion, or pacing — they're all just smarter templates.

**The product:** Upload raw footage. AI comprehends the story, emotion, and pacing. Outputs platform-ready content. Not templates. Genuine understanding-based editing.

**The moat:**
1. Proprietary dataset built from every video processed
2. Community of creators locked in by output quality
3. Brand built around the concept of AI that actually understands narrative

**Stack:** Python · Whisper · Claude Vision · FFmpeg · Remotion · Runway API  
**Window:** 18–24 months before Adobe and ByteDance close the gap  
**Build cadence:** Saturday nights. This time is protected.

## Secondary Vision: WhatsApp SME Automation

**Target:** Nigerian and West African SMEs. WhatsApp is the primary business communication tool for this market.

**The edge:** [[geographic-edge]] — no Silicon Valley founder can replicate this. You understand the workflows, the communication style, the payment tools, the infrastructure constraints.

**Revenue model:** Monthly retainer per business. Lower ticket, higher volume. Serves the immediate local market while international pipeline builds.
