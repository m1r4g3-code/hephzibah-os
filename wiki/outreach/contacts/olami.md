# Olami

Phone and gadget dealer in the Lagos electronics market (Ikeja/Yaba area). Emmanuel's go-to
supplier for device pricing, valuations, swap deals, and repairs — a running business
relationship that has warmed into genuine friendliness over four months of near-daily contact.

## What He Does

Runs (or works at) a phone/gadget shop — UK-used and open-box iPhones, Samsungs, laptops
(Dell, HP, MacBook), and PS5 handhelds are his stock-in-trade. Also handles repairs: screens,
charging ports, speakers, keyboards, battery service. Has a car vendor he can also query
(quoted a bulletproof Hilux and a Highlander for a customer of Emmanuel's in early August).
Payment channel on file: Opay 7025152733, name **Matthew Olatunde** — likely Olami's real/full
name, unconfirmed.

## The Business Relationship

Emmanuel operates as a middleman/reseller: customers come to him wanting to buy, sell, or
swap phones, and he runs prices past Olami, negotiates the spread, and closes the deal.
The entire chat is almost wall-to-wall device valuations — spot prices, swap-value math
("value the old phone, subtract from the new phone's price"), and condition checks (battery
health %, IDM/IBM/ICM screen and message status, cracked screens, Face ID working or not).
Recurring named customer: **Oyin**, who bought/swapped an iPhone 14 through this pipeline.
A shared contact, **Abbey**, comes up repeatedly as someone both of them route deals/pickups
through (Olami sent Abbey's vcard directly).

A long-running thread through April–May was a **tablet repair** ("the tab") that dragged for
weeks before Olami confirmed it was ready (5/1: "Your tab is ready").
