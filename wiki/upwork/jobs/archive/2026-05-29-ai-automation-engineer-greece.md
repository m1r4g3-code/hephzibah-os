## Job Description (Summary)

A self-described "growing AI Automation Agency" looking for an AI Automation Engineer on a "long-term basis" to build workflows, AI agents, and internal tools for their clients. Required stack: n8n, Make, Zapier, OpenAI API, Claude API, Python, prompt engineering. Nice-to-have: FastAPI, Node.js, RAG, Vector DBs, CRM integrations.

They explicitly want someone who can "design and build solutions, not just connect a few tools together."

---

## Real Problem (Diagnosis)

This is an automation agency that is building their technical bench before (or alongside) building their client base. Their $181 total spend and all-non-technical prior contracts suggest they haven't delivered a serious automation project yet — or they're doing so informally outside Upwork. They need a technical co-executor who can be the "engineering arm" of their business while they handle sales/client relationships.

The hidden risk: if they don't have steady client work, the hours won't materialize regardless of the rate agreed. The "long-term basis" language is aspirational, not guaranteed.

## Decision: BID

**Rationale:** Composite of 68 sits in the caution band, but fit score of 88 carries it — this is Emmanuel's exact niche. The real test is whether they'll pay $50/hr. Post above their range. If they negotiate to $40+ it's still worth it. If they push toward $25, that's the signal to walk — they want budget execution, not expert design.

**Positioning angle:** They said "design and build, not just connect tools." Open with a specific AI system Emmanuel architected — what it replaced, what it does, the business result. Then show the n8n or Make workflow as the visual. They've screened out most applicants already (8/9 invites unanswered) — a Loom showing a real working system closes this.

## Proposal Notes

**Diagnosis frame:** "You're building a technical practice — you need an engineer who can own systems end-to-end, not someone who needs hand-holding on every workflow."

**Proof point to use:** Most relevant n8n or AI agent project Emmanuel has built — ideally one involving multi-step AI logic or LLM integration, not just a simple Zap.

**Rate anchor:** Lead with $50/hr. If they push back, hold at $45. Below $40 = wrong client.

**Loom:** They asked for one. Record with n8n canvas or Make scenario visible. 60–90 sec. Show a real system. End with: "If this is the kind of work you're building toward — happy to jump on a 15-minute call to understand your current pipeline."

**Question to ask:** "Are your current client projects already scoped, or are you still building the pipeline?" — answers whether hours will materialize.
