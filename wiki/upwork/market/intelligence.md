## Niche Intelligence

### AI Automation / n8n / Workflow Engineering

**Demand:** High — 20-40 new jobs/week on Upwork
**Budget quality:** Variable. Serious clients: $2k–$10k. Window shoppers: $200–$500.
**Competition:** Growing but thin on quality. Most are API wrappers.
**AI saturation:** Low — the actual build work requires human judgment and client context.
**Best client types:** SaaS ops teams, agencies with repetitive processes, medical admin
**Positioning gap:** "AI-native workflow architecture" vs. "automation freelancer"
