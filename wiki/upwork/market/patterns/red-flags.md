# Red Flag Patterns

Anti-patterns from jobs, clients, or proposals that led to ghosting, bad outcomes, or wasted connects. Append-only.

---

## Hard Rules (pre-confirmed — skip immediately)

These are known bad signals. No data needed to confirm — industry wisdom + first principles.

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Payment not verified | Client hasn't committed to platform | Skip |
| 0% hire rate + 10+ posted jobs | Window shopper | Skip |
| "Trial task" or "test first" | Wants free work | Skip |
| "Per task" payment structure | Scope will creep to infinity | Skip |
| 5+ active contracts simultaneously | Won't have bandwidth for you | Skip |
| Budget < $100 for complex deliverable | Doesn't understand the scope | Skip |
| Same job reposted 3+ times | Something is wrong with their hiring process | Skip with caution |
| Average client review < 4.0 | Multiple freelancers had bad experiences | Skip |
| "Need everything done" with no spec | No real scope = JSS at risk | Skip |
