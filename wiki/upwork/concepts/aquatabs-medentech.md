## Ownership chain

Medentech is not independent — it's been rolled up into a larger group:

**Medentech Ltd** → acquired into **Kersia Group** (2018) → Kersia HQ'd in **Dinard, France** → Kersia owned by private equity firm **IK Partners** (bought from Ardian, December 2020).

So the brand name traces to an Irish manufacturer, but the controlling entity is a French-headquartered, PE-owned biosecurity/hygiene conglomerate. Kersia's portfolio spans food safety, farm biosecurity, and water purification — Aquatabs is one product line inside a much larger group.

## Findings relevant to the Giovanni/NGO context

1. **No confirmed Italian distributor found anywhere in this search.** Authorized distributors turned up for the US, Canada, Pakistan, Afghanistan — none for Italy. This doesn't prove SERAMAN or its NGO partner isn't legitimate, just that they're not publicly listed as an authorized channel the way distributors in other countries are. Worth knowing if authenticity/sourcing ever becomes a real question.
2. **Documented grey-market/counterfeit problem exists in this product category.** One real authorized distributor (Specialized Logistics Solutions, a UN Global Marketplace-registered vendor that supplied 52 million tablets for the 2024–25 South Sudan cholera response) publishes a public "how to verify genuine Aquatabs" checklist — batch codes, certificates of analysis, customs paperwork, suspiciously low pricing as a red flag. Not hypothetical; a real, acknowledged issue in the humanitarian water-purification supply chain.
