# Ramshaw Profile System

Source: "The Upwork Profile Checklist That Made Me $80,000 in 3 Months" — Ryan Ramshaw
Also draws from: "I Rank #1 For N8N On Upwork", "The Upwork Strategy I Give My Clients"
Time to complete: ~4 hours (testimonials take a few extra days to populate)

---

## The 10-Stage Checklist

### Stage 1 — Research (30 min minimum, more = better)

**Step 1:** Upwork → Search → Talent → your niche → filter Earned Amount → $10K → edit URL, add a zero → $100K+ earners.

Bookmark all of them. Study:
- Title format
- First line of description
- Portfolio structure (how many pieces, screenshot vs. video, descriptions)
- Skills listed (copy the ones relevant to your niche)
- Certifications
- Work history titles

**Step 2 (Ramshaw-exclusive):** Make a client account. Post a **fake job** in your niche — realistic, looks like a real post. Invite the top profiles you bookmarked. They apply. Read their proposals. See: how do the best people open? What examples do they use? What's their close? This gives you unlimited examples of what's currently converting.

"Out of 18 million freelancers on Upwork, there are probably fewer than 100 serious active people in your niche. We're not really competing with each other."

---

### Stage 2 — The Big Four

**(1) 100% Profile Completion**
Required before Upwork unlocks: Rising Talent, Top Rated, Top Rated Plus. No completion = no badge = algorithm disadvantage. Do this first, before anything else.

**(2) Keyword Saturation**
Keywords go in: title, description, description bottom spam, portfolio titles, portfolio descriptions, skills inside each portfolio piece (3-5 per piece), certifications (2 free ones), education, employment history, other experiences, work history contract titles (ask clients to rename them).

Ryan's numbers: "AI" appears 24 times, "automation" 18 times across his profile.

Use Ctrl+F on your profile to count. Primary keyword should appear 15-25+ times total.

**(3) Shorten Your Name**
If your name is long or difficult to pronounce for Western clients: abbreviate it. Ryan's student Jatinder → J → immediately got more messages. Emmanuel → "Hephzibah" (brand) or just "Emmanuel" works. Test if needed.

**(4) Rising Talent Badge**
Appears after 100% completion + activity. Clients don't know what it means. It looks like a badge. Badges = credibility signal. Get it.

---

### Stage 3 — Profile Picture

- Shoulder width up, camera facing you
- Natural smile (teeth showing or not — doesn't matter)
- Add a **ring/border** in Canva — makes your thumbnail stand out in search results
- Candid style is better than stiff formal headshot
- Do NOT use AI background color replacement — looks cheap
- Combined with "Available Now" badge = strong visual presence in search

---

### Stage 4 — Video Introduction

Record a Loom where you scroll through your profile and show your work.

Do this **after** the rest of the profile is built (more to show = better).

Main thing to communicate: I'm personable, I'm reliable, I can do the job. Keep it conversational. This is not a sales pitch — it is a trust builder. Same energy as your proposal Looms.

---

### Stage 5 — Profile Description

- **Do NOT use ChatGPT.** You write this once. Make it count.
- **First line** must state exactly what you do and the outcome you deliver.
  - Ryan: "Give me something to build and I'll build it so it works for you 24/7. That's my promise."
  - Emmanuel equivalent: something specific to n8n/AI automation + the outcome (saves time, eliminates manual work, runs without supervision)
- Body: specific tools, specific niches, one or two proof points
- **Bottom:** spam all relevant keywords as a list. Looks ugly, works algorithmically.

---

### Stage 6 — Skills and Keywords

Use all available skill slots. Make sure the 20 most important skills are listed. Align skills with the job categories you're targeting. Use Ctrl+F to count keyword frequency.

---

### Stage 7 — Portfolio (4 pieces minimum, 12 target)

- 4 pieces = minimum. 12 pieces = 4 pages on desktop, maximizes surface area
- Each piece: multiple images, 2-3 paragraphs, 3-5 skills tagged
- Include a Loom video walkthrough inside each portfolio piece where possible
- Variety of industries (don't put all in same category)
- Tie to testimonials — if Bob gave a testimonial, create a portfolio piece for Bob's project

---

### Stage 8 — Testimonials

Two types:

**LinkedIn testimonials (fastest social proof):**
Anyone with an aged LinkedIn account can leave a testimonial on your Upwork profile. Upwork emails them a link. 5-8 days to appear. Ask friends, family, collaborators. Minimum: 5.

**Cheap consultations ($15):**
Set up a consultation offer on your profile at $15. Client books, you do a short call, they leave a review. Each = completed contract = JSS contribution = Rising Talent → Top Rated path. This is the fastest route to early reviews with no traditional contract history.

---

### Stage 9 — Other Sections

- **Education:** Add something. Even self-taught courses. Fill the section.
- **Certifications:** Add 2 free self-created ones with your primary keywords in the names. (e.g., "n8n Automation Practitioner", "AI Workflow Design")
- **Employment history:** Add past roles or label freelance work as a position.
- All of these = additional keyword slots + section completion = algorithmic weight.

---

### Stage 10 — Settings and Launch

- "Available Now" badge: always on. 2 connects/day. Worth it.
- Rate: start competitive to get first reviews, raise after 3+ contracts
- Profile visibility: public
- uprankmir.com: check keyword rankings after profile is live

---

## The Inbound Flywheel (what all of this builds toward)

Profile → keyword rankings → profile appears in client searches → invitations arrive → Loom sent → call booked → SOW sent → close

Ryan does not send proposals now. He is fully inbound. Every month: hundreds of invitations, closes 3-5 clients, $50-80k revenue.

This is the endgame. It starts with the profile. It compounds with every completed contract.

**Emmanuel's path:** Proposals → first reviews → Rising Talent → first invitations → more proposals (now with social proof) → Top Rated → more invitations → boosting → fully inbound

## Profile Picture + Border Setup

1. Take a good photo (shoulder up, natural smile, good lighting)
2. Open Canva → create a circle with a border/ring
3. Place your photo inside the circle
4. Export as PNG with transparent background
5. Upload to Upwork
