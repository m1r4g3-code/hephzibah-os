# JSS Mechanics — How Job Success Score Works

*Research date: 2026-05-28. Sources: Upwork support docs, UpHunt, GigRadar.*

JSS is the single highest-weighted factor in Upwork's ranking algorithm (25–30%). It is the compound interest account of your Upwork career. One bad contract destroys months of work.

---

## Core Formula

```
(successful outcomes − negative outcomes) / total outcomes = JSS %
```

Upwork calculates across **three windows** — 6 months, 12 months, 24 months — and displays the highest of the three. A bad contract falls off the 6-month window after 7 months. Falls off the 24-month window after 2 years. Keep billing and bad history disappears.

Higher-value contracts carry more JSS weight than small ones.

---

## What Boosts JSS

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---|
| Public ratings 4–5 stars | High |
| Private NPS 9–10 (hidden from you) | High |
| Long-term contracts active every 90 days | Very high — each 90-day interval counts as a new successful outcome |
| Higher-value contracts | More weight than small ones |
| Client with violations/flags giving bad feedback | Upwork discounts their rating |

---

## What Hurts JSS

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---|
| Private client NPS below 7/10 | High — even a 5-star public review + 6/10 private drags JSS down |
| Contracts closing without any feedback | Neutral, NOT positive — 3 silent closings can offset 1 strong review |
| Freelancer-initiated contract endings | Strong negative regardless of mutual agreement |
| Disputes and refunds | High negative — even disputes you win (logged as communication failure) |
| Long-idle open contracts | Red flag — Upwork interprets as unresolved issues |

---

## The Rules That Protect JSS

**Rule 1: Never end a contract yourself.**
Always let the client close. If they go silent after delivery:
> "Everything is wrapped up on my end. Could you close the contract when you get a chance?"

**Rule 2: Proactively close finished contracts.**
Don't let contracts sit open after work is done. Message clients. Ask them to close. Moving JSS 5–8 points from this alone is documented.

**Rule 3: Manage the private NPS score.**
After every contract, Upwork sends clients a private satisfaction survey. You never see it. Managing the relationship temperature before close — sending a summary of what was delivered, following up, delivering a small unexpected extra — is how you engineer 9–10s instead of 7s.

**Rule 4: Never take scope-ambiguous jobs.**
"Ongoing tasks," "as needed," "other duties as required" = guaranteed dispute risk. One dispute tanks 6–12 months of JSS building. Skip it.

**Rule 5: Never take unverified-payment clients.**
Zero spend history + unverified payment = no JSS upside, guaranteed cash risk. Hard skip.

## Badge Thresholds

| Badge | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Rising Talent | No JSS required. Completeness, early feedback quality, skills demand, response rate, account standing |
| Top Rated | JSS 90%+ (unlocks after ~3+ completed contracts) |
| Top Rated Plus | JSS 90%+ AND $10,000+ earnings in past 12 months |

Below 79% JSS = very difficult to win new work. Below 90% = no badge, reduced search visibility.

**Rising Talent note:** Only active before JSS is established. After ~3 contracts with feedback, JSS activates and Rising Talent drops off automatically. Goal: earn Rising Talent during the 0-review window → badge in proposals + 30 free Connects bonus.

---

## JSS Building Strategy for New Account

1. First 3 contracts = foundation. Prioritize 5-star outcome + public written review + private NPS 9+
2. Choose clients carefully — unverified payment is a hard no
3. Pick clear-scope, fixed-budget jobs first. Scope ambiguity = dispute risk
4. Ask every client to close the contract after delivery (don't leave idle)
5. When you have a long-term client, keep the contract technically active (even 1 hour/month) — each 90 days = new positive JSS signal
6. Never compete on price to the point where you resent the work — resentment kills the private NPS
