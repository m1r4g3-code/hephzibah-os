# Concept: Automation = Permanent Asset Pricing

## The Rule

When you build an automation pipeline for a client, you are not selling labor hours or per-unit output. You are building a permanent business asset they will use indefinitely.

Price accordingly.

## The Math That Kills You

Per-unit thinking: "37 videos at $8 = $296 + $150 setup = $446"

Asset thinking: "This pipeline runs every time they launch a new product. Forever. What is that worth?"

Answer: $1,000-5,000 depending on client size. Not $446.

## The Framing That Closes It

"You pay once. You never commission videos manually again."

That sentence does more work than any feature list.

## Client Size Calibration

Not every client is a $5,000 client. Calibrate to their ceiling:

- Small Fiverr business (e.g. SavvySox, $100 original budget): $1,000-1,500
- Mid-size e-commerce or agency: $2,000-4,000
- Enterprise or high-volume brand: $5,000+

Use prior budget behavior as the signal. If they negotiated $700 → $200, they are not a $5,000 client yet. Close at $1,200, build the case study, charge $5,000 to the next one.
