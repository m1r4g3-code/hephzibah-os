# Proposal Conversion — Hard A/B Data

This node replaces intuition with data. All figures from a controlled A/B study across 10,000+ proposals, 8 months, 127 variables tested, 95% confidence threshold required, minimum 500 proposals per variant.

---

## Word Count

| Word count | Conversion rate |
|---|---|
| Under 200 words | 3.2% |
| 200-274 words | 9.1% |
| **275-325 words** | **14.7% (winner)** |
| 326-400 words | 11.2% |
| 400-500 words | 8.9% |
| 600+ words | 4.1% |

**Implication:** The 150-250 word rule optimizes for engagement-before-reading (shorter = more likely to be opened fully). The 275-325 range optimizes for conversion. The additional 50-75 words for a binary CTA, social proof placement, and one specific proof point make the difference. Apply 275-325 as the new target for context jobs.

---

## Timing After Job Posting

| Time since posting | Conversion rate |
|---|---|
| 0-1 hour | 12.8% |
| **1-3 hours** | **18.7% (winner)** |
| 3-6 hours | 14.2% |
| 6-24 hours | 10.1% |
| 24+ hours | 6.9% |

**Implication:** Bidding in the first 5-10 minutes may trigger spam signals (appears automated). The 1-3 hour window is optimal: early enough for the algorithmic boost, late enough to not appear automated. The first 2 hours remain the highest-ROI window.

## Opening Line Impact

| Opening type | Relative performance |
|---|---|
| Problem-specific observation + concrete metric | 312% higher response rate vs. generic greeting |
| Generic "I am excited about this opportunity" | Baseline (near zero) |
| First-line reference to portfolio without client context | Below baseline |

Problem-specific opening that names a concrete metric is also:
- 67% more likely to result in hire
- 23% higher project values on average

**What this looks like:** "Your checkout flow requires 7 clicks — that's costing approximately $12K/month in abandoned carts at your current traffic" vs "I am excited about this opportunity."

The specific observation + metric is the opener from the Dunford value chain applied to the proposal.

## CTA Format

| CTA type | Conversion rate |
|---|---|
| **Specific binary choice** | **19.7% (winner)** |
| "Investment + ROI" pricing framing | 17.2% |
| One binary yes/no question | 16.8% |
| Outcome clarity ("Done = [criteria]") | +15-30% lift on top of other CTAs |
| Open-ended question ("What's your vision?") | 6.1% |
| "Let me know what you think" | 2.3% |

**The binary choice CTA:** "Would Tuesday or Thursday work for a 15-minute call?" — not "let me know if you want to talk." Two specific options, one decision. Minimum cognitive load.

## Social Proof Placement

| Placement | Conversion rate |
|---|---|
| After solution, before pricing | 15.7% (winner) |
| At the beginning | 8.9% |
| After pricing | 7.2% |

**Implication:** Starting with social proof interrupts the client's problem-framing. Let them recognize their problem first, then see proof you've solved it, then see the price.

## Portfolio Links

| Link type | Conversion rate |
|---|---|
| 1 highly relevant portfolio example | 16.3% (winner) |
| 2 relevant examples | 14.1% |
| Generic "see my portfolio" | 8.2% |
| No link | 9.4% |
| 3+ links | 7.8% (diminishing returns) |

One link, highly relevant to their specific situation, beats any other configuration.

## Video (Loom) Impact

| Project size | Video effect |
|---|---|
| Projects over $5k | +127% conversion |
| Projects $1k-$5k | +47% conversion |
| **Projects under $1k** | **-73% conversion (hurts)** |

Video on small projects signals excessive effort that makes the client uncomfortable. Apply the Loom rule only to: proposals scoring 75+ OR budget confirmed at $1k+.

## Compound Effect

Optimizations compound, not add:

| Optimizations applied | Improvement |
|---|---|
| 1 change | +15-40% |
| 3 changes | +89% |
| 10+ changes | +340% |

The highest-leverage single change: the opening line with a specific observation and metric. Second: binary CTA. Third: correct word count range. These three compound to well above +100%.

## Follow-Up Cadence

| Follow-up timing | Effect |
|---|---|
| T+24 hours (next day, adds value) | +2-4 percentage points |
| T+72 hours (72h, adds new context) | +2-4 percentage points |
| "Just checking in" (no value added) | Neutral to negative |
| After client views without response | One follow-up adding specific value (expanded scope options, a relevant finding) — not generic |

The 72-hour follow-up should not be "circling back" — it should reference the specific proposal point and add one new piece of relevant context.

## Niche Benchmark Data (GigRadar)

| Niche | Reply rate range | Win rate range |
|---|---|---|
| Data & AI | 18-28% | 6-12% |
| Enterprise/Regulated | 16-24% | 6-10% |
| Growth tier ($5k-$15k) | 16-26% | 6-12% |
| Top performers (Data & AI) | 22-30% achievable | — |

**Algorithm feedback loop:** Proposal-to-interview ratio by category is tracked. High volume with low wins tells the algorithm you are a poor match. Apply to 100, win 1 = "poor match" signal. Apply to 20, win 3 = "strong match" signal. Selective bidding is algorithmic self-protection.

## Proposal Opening Categories That Drive Replies

Four categories of winning openers:

1. **Plan-First**: Reference 2 specifics from their post + offer a tiny testable first step + mark acceptance criteria ("Done = [criteria]")
2. **Proof-First**: Result + artifact beats adjectives. Mirror their project with similar results. Before/after snapshot.
3. **Question-Led**: Binary choice framing, hidden dependency check, risk reveal. Makes replying easy and shows strategic thinking.
4. **Risk-Mitigation**: Paid discovery approach, rollback promise, guaranteed clarity. Reduce client anxiety when scope is uncertain.
