# Upwork Pricing Strategy

Rate philosophy, anchor points, and how to hold price under client pressure. Pricing is positioning. The moment you discount, you signal you didn't believe you were worth it.

---

## Rate Structure

### Hourly Rate

**Starting rate:** $35–50/hr (building reputation and reviews)
**Target rate (3 months):** $60–80/hr
**Target rate (6 months):** $80–120/hr

**Rate setting logic:**
- Rate = (value delivered to client) / (hours × margin factor)
- Not: (what the market pays) × (what I'm comfortable asking)
- The limiting factor early-stage is JSS and review count, not skill

---

### Fixed-Price Anchors

| Engagement Type | Minimum | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Simple workflow (1-2 n8n nodes, single integration) | $500 | $800 |
| Medium workflow (3-5 nodes, multiple integrations) | $800 | $1,500 |
| Complex automation system (full AI agent + orchestration) | $2,000 | $4,000 |
| Full-stack web MVP (Next.js, auth, database) | $1,500 | $3,500 |
| Dashboard + data integration | $1,000 | $2,500 |
| Consulting / architecture review | $200 | $500 |

---

## The Rate Conversation

Most clients will test price. The frame to hold:

**If they say:** "Can you do it for less?"
**The response:** "I price based on the outcome delivered, not the hours spent. If the project scope is different than what I quoted, let's talk about what to de-scope. If the scope is the same, the price is the same."

**If they say:** "Another freelancer quoted half your price."
**The response:** "That's possible. The question is what they're building — not just this sprint, but what it looks like in 6 months when you need to extend it or something breaks. I build things that don't create problems later. That's priced differently."

**If they say:** "We have a tight budget."
**The response:** "I understand. What's the most important outcome from this project? Let's scope to that and see if we can work within your number."

---

## When to Walk Away on Price

Walk away when:
- Client wants to negotiate below the minimum rate for the engagement type
- Client wants to "start small to test you" with no commitment to the larger project
- Client expects equity or profit-sharing instead of payment
- Client wants milestone payments lower than 50% upfront

Never do spec work or trial tasks without payment. The `middleman-lesson` is the rule.

---

## Rate Increase Triggers

Increase rates when:
- JSS reaches 90%+
- 3+ 5-star reviews on profile
- Proposal reply rate reaches 20%+
- Multiple clients complimenting delivery quality in messages
