## How to Update

`/strategy-review` runs weekly and:
1. Reads `metrics.md`
2. Reads last 30 days of proposal outcomes
3. Checks for patterns (N≥3 with shared characteristic + shared outcome)
4. Writes confirmed patterns here
5. Writes weekly strategy note

Never delete old entries. Old patterns that are later contradicted: mark as "superseded" with the date.
