# Account Launch — Own Account Cold Start

Emmanuel's own Upwork account (`~011b48d2eabbfa6361`) needs to be built from zero.
This playbook is the exact sequence to execute when the account goes live.
Rate floor: $40/hr minimum. No reviews. No JSS. Starting with portfolio, keywords, and Loom.

Nothing from the partner account transfers. This account wins or loses on its own.

## Pre-Launch Research (Do This Before Touching the Profile)

1. Upwork search → click "Talent" → type "n8n automation"
2. Filter by Earned Amount → $10K+ → edit URL to see $100K+ earners
3. Bookmark 5-7 top profiles. Study: title format, overview structure, portfolio thumbnails, keyword count
4. Also: create a fake client account → post a job in AI automation → read what proposals come in. This is what Emmanuel's profile competes against.

> "Great art is stolen. Copy the top profiles."

## Day 0 — Profile Foundation (3-4 hours, do in one session)

### 1. Profile Picture
- Shoulder-width up, natural smile, good lighting
- Have someone else take it — candid beats posed
- After uploading: add a ring/border in Canva around the profile picture thumbnail
- This makes the thumbnail stand out in search results
- Do NOT use a fake background color — looks low quality

### 2. Display Name
Emmanuel is fine. No shortening needed — it's pronounceable for English-speaking clients.

### 3. Title (write this last, after reviewing top earner profiles)
Formula: `[Primary Keyword] + | + [Stress removal framing]`

Current candidate:
> "AI Automation Engineer | n8n workflows that run while your team focuses on real work"

Rules:
- Primary keyword must appear in title
- Second half speaks to a problem/pain — not a capability
- Read it from a busy business owner's perspective: does this sound like stress removal?
- Test: would a client stressed about manual repetitive work click this? If yes, it works.

### 4. Overview/Bio
Write it yourself. Do not use ChatGPT. Read aloud test: if you wouldn't say it to someone, cut it.

Structure:
```
Line 1: Outcome + implicit promise
  "I build automation systems that remove the manual work your team is drowning in — n8n, AI agents, API integrations, whatever the stack needs."

Body (3-4 short paragraphs):
  - What types of projects you take
  - One or two specific examples (SERAMAN pipeline, SavvySox automation are real)
  - What working with you looks like (fast delivery, documented outputs, approval gates)

Bottom block (keyword spam — works, Ramshaw verified):
  n8n | AI Automation | Workflow Automation | API Integration | OpenAI | Claude AI |
  CRM Automation | Make.com | Zapier | Webhook Integration | AI Agent | Chatbot |
  Business Process Automation | HeyGen | Video Automation | Content Automation
```

## Days 1-14 — Portfolio Build (non-negotiable, do before first proposal)

Target: 12 pieces minimum. Ramshaw: "5x more likely to get hired with a good portfolio section."

### Each piece requires:
- Multiple images (not just one)
- 2-3 paragraphs: what was the brief, what did you build, what was the result
- Keyword-first title: "n8n Lead Qualification Workflow — SaaS Agency" not "My Automation Project"
- 5 skills tagged per piece (more keyword coverage)
- Branded thumbnail (consistent with profile picture style — same colors, same aesthetic)

### Starting pieces (real work Emmanuel has done):
1. **SERAMAN Social Automation Pipeline** — 6-7 workflow n8n system, Italian tactical gear brand, approval gates, HeyGen video, multi-platform publish
2. **SavvySox Hologram Product Automation** — Google Drive trigger → n8n → Kie AI hologram (black bg, 3D rotate, neon glow) → Gmail notification. 15-sec per sock, zero manual work
3. **Any other real builds** — document them all

### Filling the remaining pieces (if real work is thin):
- Build sample projects (document them properly, same standard as real work)
- Get a friend/contact to let you automate something for free → screenshot it → portfolio piece
- Repurpose SERAMAN phases as separate pieces if they're meaningfully different

### Spread across industries:
Do NOT make all 12 pieces about AI automation for fashion brands. Spread:
- SaaS (CRM automation)
- E-commerce (product automation)
- Agency (content pipeline)
- Healthcare (scheduling/intake)
- Real estate (lead qualification)
Even if the underlying tech is the same n8n — frame each piece in the client's industry language.

### Tie portfolio to testimonials:
When a LinkedIn testimonial is submitted for Person X, create a portfolio piece for Person X's project. The two reinforce each other.

## Days 1-7 — LinkedIn Testimonials (start immediately, parallel to portfolio)

Target: 5 testimonials before first proposal batch.

Process:
1. Identify 5 people with aged LinkedIn accounts (not just created)
2. Get: first name, last name, business email, LinkedIn profile URL
3. In Upwork profile settings → add testimonial → enter their info → Upwork emails them
4. Appears on your profile in 5-8 days
5. If not appeared after 8 days: open Upwork support ticket, ask them to push it through

Who to ask:
- Friends who know you can build things (even if they haven't hired you)
- Anyone who has seen the SERAMAN or other work
- Family members with a business context

## Day 21+ — First Proposal Batch

By now:
- Profile is 100% complete
- 12 portfolio pieces are live
- 5 testimonials submitted (some may have appeared already)
- Available Now badge is on
- Keywords have been Ctrl+F counted on the live profile (primary keyword should appear 10+ times)
- Rate: $40/hr minimum. No exceptions. No "just this once" discounts.

### The first review strategy:
The first few contracts are review acquisition, not revenue optimization. A $40/hr 10-hour project ($400) with a 5-star review is worth more than a $2k project that drags on and risks a JSS hit.

Accept smaller scopes at the rate. Deliver fast. Ask directly:
> "Everything is wrapped up on my end. Would you be able to take a moment to leave me feedback on the contract? It really helps at this stage."

One 5-star review changes the profile's credibility signal. Two puts you in a different tier. Three and the flywheel starts.

## Rate Ladder (own account)

| Milestone | Rate |
|---|---|
| Launch, 0 reviews, Rising Talent pending | $40/hr |
| First 5-star review | $45/hr |
| 3rd review + JSS visible | $50/hr |
| Top Rated badge | $60/hr |
| Top Rated Plus (after $10k+ earned) | $75/hr+ |

Do not negotiate down from the floor. If $40/hr is too high for a job, skip the job.
Rate signals quality. A $20/hr rate on a fresh account signals "uncertain." $40/hr signals conviction.
